# CAMP-FUNERAL-GB — First-call-to-aftercare case OS

Status: DISTILLED | Template: tpl-funeral-gb | Teardown: td-funeral-gb | Pattern: case-led

## 1. Market overview
- PlotBox dominates (first-call → case → custody → scheduling → contracts → family portal; Funeral Director Portal; UK G-Cloud). Independents largely on whiteboards — the single-source gap IS the opportunity.
- CMA Order 2021 is hard law: Standardised + Attended/Unattended Price Lists, ownership disclosure, hospital/hospice/care-home inducement ban, coroner/police solicitation ban. NO licensing regime exists in England/Wales (recommended only) — never claim regulated status.

## 2. Acquisition
- At-need first calls (often out-of-hours — answer always), pre-need plans maturing, care-home/hospice/coroner relationships (strictly non-inducement).

## 3. Offer catalog
- Attended/unattended funerals per CMA price definitions, disbursements itemised separately, pre-need plans (TBD: local bands).

## 4. Unit economics
- Professional fees vs disbursements tracked separately from first entry; aftercare feeds pre-need pipeline.

## 5. Supply/operations strategy
- QR-scanned custody chain (offline-capable); arrangement packs prepared, director-led; multi-party scheduling with double-booking guards.

## 6. Distribution strategy
- Aftercare programme (care + pre-need pipeline); community presence; price-transparency pages exceeding CMA minimums.

## 7. Listing structure
- Google profile; CMA-compliant price lists published; Funeral Director Portal presence.

## 8. Landing/conversion structure
- First call (structured) → transfer (custody scan) → arrangement (director + pack) → service → disbursements → invoice → aftercare.

## 9. Success metrics + kill rules
- First-call answer %, arrangement conversion, disbursement dispute count (target 0), aftercare→pre-need conversion.
- Kill: any custody gap or disbursement dispute → stop, review, fix before resume.
