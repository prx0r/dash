## BreadPacks: Template Businesses

A pack = a complete executable business strategy:

```yaml
name: Dark Academia Botanical Prints

world:
  niche: dark_academia_home_decor

products:
  source: public_domain_botanical_art
  production: print_on_demand

channels:
  - etsy
  - shopify
  - pinterest

audience:
  interests:
    - dark academia
    - victorian interiors
    - botanical illustration

economics:
  target_aov: 48
  min_gross_margin: 0.58
  max_cac: 12

scanners:
  - pinterest_trend_scanner
  - competitor_price_scanner
  - creative_performance_scanner

ads:
  pinterest:
    daily_budget: 10

automation:
  listing: autonomous
  repricing: approval
  ads: bounded
```

Someone who is excellent at Pinterest builds this once, sells it to 500 people.

**That person is selling a business program.**
